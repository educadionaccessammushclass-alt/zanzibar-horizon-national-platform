Independent governance

Programme decisions should be supported by documented criteria, independent assessment, conflict-of-interest declarations and clear oversight responsibilities.

Safeguarding

Children and vulnerable people must be protected through safeguarding policies, informed consent, responsible referrals, restricted access and appropriate incident response procedures.

Privacy and data protection
